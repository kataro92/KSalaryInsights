## English

**App name**  
KVSalaryTools

**Subtitle**  
Estimate pay · tax · SI offline

**Short description**  
Estimate Gross↔Net pay, annual PIT settlement and SI benefits — offline on device.

**Full description**

KVSalaryTools helps you see every payroll deduction and tax line using year/period rulesets (2025 & 2026).

• Gross ↔ Net, dependents, monthly bonus, overtime (including night OT)  
• Annual settlement + authorize vs self-file wizard  
• Benefits: severance, unemployment, maternity, sick leave, pension / lump sum  
• Other income: rent, household business (quick estimate), securities, ESOP, casual pay  

Ngài Miu guides each step. Calculations and scenarios stay on device — no national ID, tax ID or SI book required. Salary figures are never uploaded.

Estimates only — not legal/accounting advice or an official filing tool.
