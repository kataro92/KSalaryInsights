## English

**App name** 
KSalaryInsights

**Subtitle** 
Estimate pay · tax · SI offline

**Short description** 
Estimate Gross↔Net pay, annual PIT settlement and SI benefits. offline on device.

**Full description**

KSalaryInsights helps you estimate pay, tax and insurance when comparing job offers, checking a payslip, or when rates change by year. Every step shows the formula, with no black box.

**When to use it**
• Compare Gross vs Net offers (e.g. 28M Net vs 32M Gross), including SI on base / partial / full pay 
• Understand why take-home is lower than quoted; split insurance, relief and bracket tax to match the payslip 
• Sanity-check employer figures when SI caps / relief / PIT brackets change (2025 & 2026) without accounting expertise 
• Estimate annual settlement before filing; authorize vs self-file guidance 
• Benefits: severance, unemployment, maternity, sick leave, pension / lump sum 
• Other income: rent, household business, securities, ESOP, casual pay 
• Coming soon: multi-source annual tax summary on device 
• Out of scope: crypto / digital-asset tax 

**Tools**
• Gross ↔ Net, dependents, monthly bonus, overtime (including night OT) 
• Annual settlement + authorize vs self-file wizard 
• SI / Labor Code benefits as above 
• Other income: VAT and PIT on separate lines, with thresholds 

Ngài Miu guides each step. Calculations and scenarios stay on device. no national ID, tax ID or SI book required. Salary figures are never uploaded.

Estimates only. not legal/accounting advice or an official filing tool.
